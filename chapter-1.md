CHAPTER 1

Somewhere far away, a voice was trying to ruin my life.

      "JAIDEN."

I pulled the blanket over my head. Blankets, in my experience, fix most problems. They made annoying voices muffled, far away, and entirely ignorable.

       "JAIDEN MARLOCK."

       Ah— Full name. The blanket’s armor had cracked.

        "If you aren't out of that bed in five seconds," the woman said, her voice hovering directly over me, "I'm coming in. And I swear to the World Core that when I do it will not be gentle."

         I cracked one eye open, the world blooming in a jagged, half‑-dark glare. She stood in the doorway, arms crossed like a shield, her face a mask of disappointment—worse than her angry face. You could wait out angry. Disappointed just hovered over your shoulder all day like a miserable ghost.

           "Five," she said, voice flat and unyielding.

            I sat up so fast that the room spun, the ceiling tilting like a spinning top. "I'm up! Look! Fully awake now, Sofia!" I shouted, the words tasting sweeter in my own head. The woman shouting at me is Sofia Marlock, my caretaker or maid I guess. She didn’t look one bit impressed by my antics, though. Then I looked up at her broken halo, and I remembered what she said when I asked her about it.



                                                                *   *   *

           “Why is your halo broken? Did something break it?” I asked, then saw a hurt look on her face that she tried to hide.

          “It’s nothing, it’s something of the past,” she said, changing the subject as if she was uncomfortable talking about it.

          “But what of these black spots that are also on your 4 wings?” I asked, still curious.

          “JUST LET ME BE! Please,” she said, tears dropping from her face, and walked out.

*   *   *

             Since that day I have not asked about it again, not wanting to see her in tears again, though from what I could tell her halo is not actually damaged, rather it’s broken; and she does not look like the other angels I see in town. Remembering her in tears again, I decide against asking her about it. 

           "Do you know what day it is?" she said, bringing me back from memory.

           Of course I knew. Everyone in the realm knew. It was the one day no fifteen‑-year‑-old could dodge; it can be said it is comparable to being as important as your birthday or wedding. But Sofia liked stating the obvious just to make it real, so I let her.

 "Your awakening," she said. "You're due at the Elyndor Academy altar before the ninth bell, and you're still buried in sheets." The academy loomed behind the curtains, a stone citadel of whispered power where every race dared to tread, yet even the realm’s rulers could not command its gates.

Well, it is time to get off this bed and hurry for the ceremony. The first thing I did—the thing I did every morning—was reach for my pendant.

It hung on a silver chain against my collarbone: a dark ruby, cool to the touch. I’d worn it so long it felt less like jewelry and more like a limb. I never took it off. Not to sleep, not to swim, not ever. Sofia had made me promise, back when I was still small enough to ask ridiculous questions, and each time I tried to remove it, I felt a violent pain in my heart and a faint feeling that was telling me not to break my promise.

*   *   *

            “Why do I always have to keep this on?” I said while clutching the pendant.

             “Because it keeps you safe, and you gave me your word,” she told me, her eyes dead serious.

             “From what?”             “From —”

*   *   *

 Pretty sure I don’t remember giving anyone my word at six-years-old, but I’d kept the promise anyway, not wanting to experience that pain of trying to remove it ever again. Gripping my fingers around the cold gem through my shirt, letting it anchor me for what is to come today. Still, what was she trying to say? I just can’t remember, but it seemed important, and each time I ask about what the pendant is protecting me from, she pretends not to have heard me and continues with whatever she is doing.

*   *   *

            After I left my room for breakfast, which was a cooked meat made over corrupt-veyn fire, and a starfruit which looks like a luminous fruit and tastes like honey, I swallowed it without thinking much, since each time I eat them I feel much more alive than when I secretly tried eating regular food.

“Why do you always hide when Bella’s parents come over to pick her up?”

“I am busy when they come over so I never had the chance to do so.”

“What do you mean, busy? You barely do much work — at least, none that I know of.”

“Let’s just say I always am,” she said, sounding annoyed by my remark, and her halo brightened again before dimming.

“Sure. I also heard her parents insulting you while they hurried to take Bella away. They warned her — then warned her again — not to come here. Though she still always did. Typical her." I shook my head.

             “Are you not meant to be somewhere now?” she said, sounding more uncomfortable than ever.

“JAIDEN MARLOCK!” 

Someone shouted it from outside the house. I already knew who it was. I started racing to finish eating, grabbed the starfruit, and shrugged my school uniform on — white, black-edged, the badge close to the collar.           “I’M COMING IN A FEW SECONDS!” I shouted back at Bella.

Across the table, Sofia just watched me, her broken halo flickering a little before dimming again. She’d looked at me like that my whole life—the way you watch a kid standing too close to a ledge. Not hovering, but ready to grab your shirt if you slipped.

“Oh, gosh, we are so late, we need to start running,” running out to see Bella, who looks just as anxious as me because of today. I take a look at Bella who is also wearing the academy’s uniform and the uniform has space where her 6 wings are.

“I can’t wait for my class and halo to form! What class do you think I might get?” Bella said excitedly.

“You’re enjoying this too much, take us to the academy before we are known for the 2 kids who missed their class assignments and become useless people who can’t even get a job.”            “The sound of a stress-free life with no job sounds nice, though,” she said playfully, then picked me up, heading towards the academy. As we are getting near, I start to see that the place is already packed with lots of people.

          “HEY WATCH IT!” someone shouted at us.          “We’re sorry, we are in kind of a hurry,” Bella said, talking to a man with light skin and pointed ears — a light elf called a Pallantine elf — who was riding a Griffen to another section of the academy. He grumbled something before continuing to fly off to where he was going before.

           “Finally, we are here and we made it just in time before they started,” I said, looking down as we started descending to the school gates, seeing people entering the school gates in 2 directions. The academy looks more impressive than how it looked from my window, still looking like how it was from my window.             “Still can’t believe we actually get to enroll into such a prestigious school,” taking a bite into my starfruit as we land and I hurriedly finished it.

           “ACADEMY STUDENTS USE THE WEST GATES FOR THE ACADEMY-EXCLUSIVE Sundering Altar, WHILE THOSE WHO ARE NOT ACADEMY STUDENTS GO TO THE EAST GATE, WHICH IS FOR THE commoners.” someone in robes shouted Infront of the school.

          “Alright, come on, Bella, let’s be going now,” dragging her along the other students who are also wearing the same uniform.

  Flap smack!

         “OUCH, hey, watch your wings, they got in my face,” rubbing my face where it got hit.

         “Oh, sorry, didn’t see you there, it just felt cramped keeping it still,” the girl said, apologizing. As she is heading inside, I looked closely and saw she had 4 wings, which signified that she is an angel of Virtue rank, though I still don’t know what rank Bella is. Wait, I can’t believe I didn’t think of this before: if that girl is a Virtue, then Sofia is also a Virtue angel too, right?

         “Hey Bella, what angel rank are you?” turning to her as she stops looking around admiringly to look at me, and her 6 wings spread out a little.

         “Oh umm, it’s Aetherblade Lord.”

*   *   *



        Entering the hall where the assessment would be happening, the hall could hold at least a thousand people. A semi‑circular stage faced the door, surrounded by seats. White walls with intricate pillars framed the room. In the center of the stage lay a strange altar, its runes and alien script, which I think might be the altar that would read the class and other details. We took our seats together.

        “This place is better than what my brother told me in his letters,” someone said to his friend. Looking at who said it, I noticed he was a demon with 4 horns and 4 wine-purple-colored wings.

        “Yeah, I hope I get a good class too.”

       “My brother’s class is Bladesinger, under the guild.”

       “Really? I thought that rank is for second-rank demons called Bloodward, which has 6 horns and 6 wings as a sign to indicate their rank.”

       “Yeah, the 2nd rank is called Bloodward, but that is not his race.”

       “I already know that. So what rank are you then if your brother is a Bloodward?”

       “I’m a 3rd-ranked demon, the rank is called Scourge. That’s why I have 4 horns and 4 wings.”

Though I would like to admire the place more, I want to talk to Bella more about what I just realized.

       “That’s cool — from what Sofia taught me, that rank are angels with 6 wings, which is the 2nd highest rank in the angel realm. But I thought those ranks are for soldiers, so are you going to be in the army too?”       “Oh lord, please no. I want nothing to do with the army,” she said, looking irritated while looking at the podium as people settle down.       “Why? What’s wrong with the army?” she turns to me, looking baffled at the question.

       “Why would I, of all people, want to join the army?”

      “I don’t know, that’s why I asked you.”

      “Not all angels in the Aetherblade Lord rank want to be soldiers, so some want to be normal, like a civilian like me, though you already know my parents are in charge of the guards in the angel realm,” she said nonchalantly, then someone in robes started walking up to the podium to officially start the event.

      “My name is Grin Holly. I will now begin this event by calling your names in order. If you are human or elf, let the magic flow through you so you can be examined for your class and other details. If you are an angel, the Fifth Pillar, Thalor Elwin, will assist you with your class and halo formation,” Mr Grin announced. As soon as he announced the Fifth Pillar’s name, everyone started to murmur, though I don’t understand why.      “Why is everyone murmuring?” folding my hand into a cup and whispering in Bella’s ears.

      “Because it is a rare sight for one of the 6 Pillars of angels that guard the angel ruler and the angel realm to be seen in public, I at least know that some are tasked with other duties other than guarding the ruler” she said, explaining it to me.

    Feeling even more nervous than before, I reach for my necklace, still feeling its cool touch, calming down a little, though it was short lived as a violent surge of conflict rose within me; I had been so focused and excited by today’s event that I had even forgotten about this issue I had been having since I had this necklace.

        “Hey Jaiden, are you okay? You look pale,” she asked worriedly.

        “Sorry, it’s nothing, just felt a little bit sick, that’s all,” brushing it off as I try to calm it down, clutching the necklace.

          “You sure? I have known you since we were kids, so I know when you are lying.”

True, I won’t deny that fact, so it really is hard to hide things from her. But I don’t have time to explain, as I was cut off by Mr Grin announcing the first name for the class readings, as we both turned to pay attention to Mr Grin.

       “Leven Merun, please come up to the stage for your assessment,” Mr Grin announced, as a dark-skinned boy with pointed ears came onto the stage and was in front of the altar. As he stood onto the circle, a bright flash of light lit the room before vanishing, then the reading came in a bright pale blue color above him.

     “Wow, did you guys see? Does that mean he won’t be able to do magic?” someone murmured to his friend next to him, but Leven paid them no mind.

      “Hey Bella, is a Ren-class really that bad?” still feeling a little bit of tightening in my chest as I still hold on to the necklace.

      “Well, from one of the soldiers, they said that it is not that it is bad, but it burns the user’s vitality, but it can be recovered with proper rest and food, though I still don’t know what Ren even is,” she said, turning to face me again.

      “Oh, I hope I don’t get a Ren-class, it feels too scary to know I would be using my own vitality to fight or do whatever the class I get does,” shivering at the thought of it. I also realized that the ache in my chest had stopped.

     “Look, the next person is also getting their reading,” she said, now looking at the podium where an angel’s reading is taking place and Thalor Elwin.

     “It’s that girl that hit me with her wings. I didn’t get to hear her name — what a bummer,” dejected, I watch her as Thalor is telling her something to say and she repeats after him. After a minute her halo forms and her reading comes next in the same bright pale blue color.

     Ok, so her name was Ivy. Every minute of waiting for my turn would wound my nerves tighter. At some point a stir ran through the hall — Princess Eva Aldermere Divina, the human realm ruler’s daughter — and just before sleep took me, I heard her class read out: Mage. The crowd was already gushing over her. I couldn’t tell how long I was out, but I woke up when they called for Bella.

        “Bella Ruven, please come up for your reading,” Mr Grin announced.

        “Well, wish me luck, I guess. Can’t wait for my halo, though,” she said, looking half excited and half worried as she stood up and was heading to the podium.

         “Yeah, wish you luck.” On the podium, Thalor did the same as he did for Ivy, and after she finished, her halo formed. A bright flash came, but this time it was different — it was brighter than before. I swear, if you kept looking at the light you would have become blind.

        “Ahhh, my eyes!” Why was it so bright? I feel like my fricking eyes would have melted from how bright it was if I kept looking at it. Her reading came afterward in the usual color.

         “Why was this brighter than all the others, like the last guy’s own, more than normal” as they were talking about Bella’s strange light, I noticed 4 pairs of eyes on me, one came from the podium and the other came from a hooded man at the door before he turned to look at Bella and left. 

    Who are those people, and why are they looking at me? And why was that hooded man looking at me before looking at Bella? It’s as if we were being monitored. That’s so weird and creepy as hell; I think I now have stalkers. But as I was in my thoughts, I had not noticed Bella had returned, way too excited about her halo.

          “I finally have my halo, Jaiden — that means I can finally do magic,” she said, way too excitedly, as she was touching her halo and grinning from ear to ear. As I was about to tell her about the people that were monitoring us, I was called to the podium for my reading.

          “Jaiden Marlock! Please come to the podium for your reading,” Mr Grin announced.

Oh, boy, it’s finally my turn, but I won’t lie and say I’m confident to walk up the stage for this with like all these people staring at me like I am an art in a museum. I don’t know what to do and just freeze there, forgetting how to even move.

           “What are you doing?” Bella whispered to me. But me, myself, I don’t even know.
